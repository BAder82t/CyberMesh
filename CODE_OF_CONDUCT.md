# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on
the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private
information are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/BAder82t/CyberMesh/security/advisories/new)
(mark the report "Code of conduct"). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove contributions and restrict participation in response to violations, following the
Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
